Tapping “I've made the payment” logs the claim and alerts your team to verify the bank transfer — it never marks itself paid.

Two scenarios, one flow:
  • Deposit was paid (shown) → bills the remaining balance (quote − deposit), with the Quote Total / Deposit Paid rows visible.
  • No deposit paid → bills the full quote; those two rows are hidden and “Balance Due” is the whole amount.

Note: the pay page & email use the invoice number as the bank payment reference, while the SMS/WhatsApp also show the booking ref (RMV-…) as a friendly identifier. Say the word if you'd like those unified.
